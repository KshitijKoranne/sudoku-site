Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

The short version

Sudoku Pro: Daily Puzzles does not collect, store or share any personal data about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your device

Your current game, settings, statistics, streak and reminder time are saved only on your device.

Apple services

  • Game Center. If you sign in, Apple handles your Game Center profile. The app sends your best solve times and achievements to Game Center. I do not receive them.
  • In-app purchase. Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. I never see your payment details.
  • Notifications. The daily reminder is a local notification on your device. It is off until you turn it on.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
