Privacy

Support

Write to kshitij.koranne@icloud.com. Tell me your device and iOS or macOS version. I usually reply within two days.

Restore Pro

Open Settings in the app, then tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.

The daily puzzle

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes at midnight on your device. A streak counts any local day on which you solve at least one puzzle.

Reminders

Turn the daily reminder on in Settings and pick a time. If nothing arrives, check Notifications for the app in your device Settings.

Game Center

Times reach the leaderboards only for puzzles solved without hints. Sign in to Game Center in your device Settings.

Lost progress

Games and stats live on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
